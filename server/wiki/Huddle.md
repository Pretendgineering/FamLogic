# Huddle: family meetings

A family meeting with a little structure: a parent writes a short agenda, the family clicks
through it together (on the TV, a tablet or the web console), hands out jobs or asks for
volunteers, and keeps a few notes. When it's over, the jobs go onto the Docket.

Switch on **Huddle** in Settings → Modules.

## Making a meeting

Parents make meetings in the app (the **Meetings** tile), on the console's **Huddle** page, or by
asking Steward: "set up a family meeting for Sunday about the holidays and this week's chores;
Sam does the bins".

An agenda is written like this, one topic per slide:

```
## Weekend plans
The park on Saturday, or the pool if it rains?
- Pack the picnic @Sam
- Bring the football

## Chores for the week
Who takes which job this week?
- Hoover the stairs
- Feed the cat @Mia
```

- `## ` starts a topic (a slide). The lines under it are what the slide says.
- `- ` is a job. Add `@Name` to give it to someone; leave it off to ask for a volunteer.
- `> ` is a note.

## Running the meeting

Open the meeting and choose **Start** (on the TV: the **Meeting** button down the right side;
switch it on in the TV's Settings). Then, on each topic:

- **Next** and **Back** move between topics (on the console, the arrow keys too).
- Choose a job to give it to someone, put it back to "needs a volunteer", or remove it.
- **Add a job** and **Add a note**: type them, or on the TV press **Speak** and say them into
  the remote.

At the end, **Send jobs to the Docket**: every job that has someone goes onto the Docket's
**From family meetings** list, for that person, once. Jobs still waiting for a volunteer stay on
the meeting.

## Where it's kept

Each meeting is a note in the vault's `Meetings/` folder, in the same format as above, so it's
also the minutes: open it in Obsidian (or any editor) to read or change it. A job that's gone to
the Docket is marked 📋.

## Building a meeting

Press the buttons to add sections, in any order: **Wins and thank-yous** (each person's is added during the meeting), **Coming up** (next week's events from the Datebook, filled in for you), **Jobs to hand out** (give each to someone, or ask for a volunteer), **A vote** (choices everyone taps; on the TV, pick a choice and then who's voting) and **A topic**. Move sections up or down, or remove them. It's all still a note in the vault.
