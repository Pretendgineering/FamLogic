# Homestead: looking after the home

Tick what your home has (a furnace, smoke alarms, gutters, a lawn...) and which days the bins go out. Homestead keeps the matching repeating chores in Upkeep, and bin days on the calendar, by itself. Untick something and its chores stop. Each chore's history (who did it, when) is the log.

- **How often:** each job has a usual frequency (change the furnace filter every 3 months). If that doesn't suit your home, change it right under the switch, to any number of days, weeks or months.
- **Your own jobs:** add anything that isn't on the list ("Bleed the radiators, every 12 months").
- **More than one place:** a grandparent's flat, a cabin, a barn or a workshop. Press **+ Another property**, give it a name, and set it up the same way. Its chores are labelled with its name. Removing a property stops its chores; the family's own home always stays.

Parents set up the home; everyone sees the upkeep and can tick it off.
