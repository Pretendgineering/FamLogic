# Dossier: people, pets and contacts

Everything you'd want a babysitter, a grandparent or a pet sitter to know, in one place.

- **People:** each person's birthday, allergies, medicines, sleep, likes and dislikes, sizes, doctor and emergency contacts. Everyone can read them (it's emergency information); parents can change anyone's, older children and grandparents their own.
- **Pets:** what kind, food, walks, medicines, the vet and the microchip number.
- **Contacts:** the family's address book: Grandma, the vet, the plumber, with their phone, email and address.

In the web console the list is on the left and the one you picked on the right: **Change** to fill it in, **Print** for a one-page sheet. **Print everyone** prints each person on their own page. **+ Add a pet** and **+ Add a contact** ask for a name (and a picture) and then open it to fill in. In the app, Dossier has the same three tabs.

Each one is a note in the vault (People, Pets and Contacts folders), so Steward and Obsidian can read and change them too.
